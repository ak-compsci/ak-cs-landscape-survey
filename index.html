This map combines two data sources to picture computer science (CS) education across Alaska's school districts. 2024-25 course data reflects district reports to AK DEED — showing schools that offered CS or CS-adjacent courses that year. Survey data (2025-26) comes from the voluntary Alaska CS Landscape Survey, capturing richer detail on current offerings, grade bands, enrollment, and barriers — but is not yet complete for all districts.
