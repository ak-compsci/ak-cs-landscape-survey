This dashboard visualizes responses from the Alaska CS Landscape Survey, administered by the Alaska Department of Education & Early Development (DEED). It maps which districts currently offer computer science courses, the grade bands served, student enrollment figures, and the most commonly reported barriers to CS instruction. The goal is to provide a clear, data-driven picture of CS education across the state so DEED can direct resources, professional development, and funding where they are needed most.


Computer science (CS) education includes courses and instruction in coding, programming, computational thinking, robotics, game design, web development, cybersecurity, data science, and artificial intelligence.
